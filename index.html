TOOLKIT GUIDE

A QUICK GUIDE TO YOUR CALCULATORS
OSC Calculator

Choose X or Z, enter the cannon and target coordinates on that axis, then calculate the distance and required blocks. Negative coordinates work too.

Swap Coordinates

Switches the cannon and target values. It changes the calculator inputs, not your actual Minecraft cannon.

Block Calculator

Converts your number of stacks and extra blocks into a total block count. It does not assume every block equals one unit of actual OSC travel.

Calculation Results

Your result appears in a popup. Copy it for later, or close the popup to return to your inputs.

Coordinates must be entered manually. The website cannot automatically read your Minecraft world position.
